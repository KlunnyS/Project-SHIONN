## Research and history

Dated audits, reviews and experiment protocols record contemporaneous evidence.
A newer maintained guide may supersede their proposed commands or next steps.
Keep hashes, exclusions and negative results; do not treat historical proposals
as completed experiments. Older broad experiments remain in the [model roadmap](MODEL_ROADMAP.md).

The [research roadmap](SHIONN_Goal_Directed_Navigation_Roadmap_v2.md) is a proposal,
not proof of implementation or gameplay outcomes. Superseded chamber/protocol
content is preserved under [archive/navigation](archive/navigation/README.md).
