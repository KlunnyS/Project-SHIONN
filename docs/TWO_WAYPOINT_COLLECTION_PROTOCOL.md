# Two-waypoint collection protocol

Use the maintained [fresh paired-goal collection procedure](PAIRED_VISUAL_GOAL_COLLECTION_2026_11_15.md)
for registration, first-pair A/B commands, validation and later-group release.
See [registered starts](REGISTERED_STARTS.md) for reset tolerances and metadata.

A is the right floor-button approach area; B is the left cube approach area.
Success is entry into the requested region. Neither interaction nor settled stop
is required. Current captures remain development-only until explicitly reviewed.

The superseded procedures are retained as [protocol history](archive/navigation/TWO_WAYPOINT_COLLECTION_PROTOCOL_HISTORY_2026_11_15.md).
